# Closing Gap WebP Asset Upload Guide

Upload final brand assets to this folder:

`public/assets/closing-gap/`

Use WebP where possible. Logos and certification marks should have transparent backgrounds if the original artwork supports it.

| `brand-logo-light.webp` | 640x180 | White/light version of the full logo for dark green sections. |

## Notes

- Keep each WebP ideally under 350 KB for logos/certifications and under 900 KB for large photos.
- Use sharp, bright, real business imagery. Avoid dark, blurry, overly cropped, or stock-looking visuals.
- Do not include private client data, dashboards, personal phone numbers, or confidential documents inside images.
